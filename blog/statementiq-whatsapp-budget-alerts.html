StatementIQ Next: WhatsApp Bank Statement Analysis + Smart Budget Alerts

By Nitesh Kumar Singh · Founder, StatementIQ · August 12, 2026

Quick answer: Two new StatementIQ features are being built for India — WhatsApp analysis (send PDF → FinHealth score in ~1 minute) and smart budget + EMI alerts (not just past analysis). The web product is already live at statementiq.in — first report free.

Most people in India already live on WhatsApp. They do not want a long website form before they understand their money. StatementIQ is adding the path they already use — plus alerts that help before the next EMI bounce or food-delivery overspend.

Why These Two Features Matter

Today StatementIQ already turns a bank PDF/Excel into a FinHealth score, plain-English insights, Hinglish AI chat, and a PDF report. That is strong for people who open the site.

Two gaps still block popularity in India:

  1. Upload friction — many users hesitate to visit a website and upload a statement.
  2. Past-only insight — a score of last month is useful, but people also need a nudge for next week.

WhatsApp + smart alerts close both gaps. That is how a one-time analyser becomes a daily money assistant.

1. WhatsApp Integration — FinHealth Score in 1 Minute

In India, WhatsApp is the default inbox. StatementIQ will offer an official WhatsApp number. You send your bank statement PDF (and the PDF password if the bank file is locked). An AI chatbot replies with:

  • Your FinHealth score (0–100)
  • A short summary — salary, EMI, UPI, food, bounces
  • A link to unlock the full report on statementiq.in

Target time: about 1 minute, not a 20-minute website session.

How the WhatsApp flow will work

  1. Save StatementIQ’s official WhatsApp number (announced on statementiq.in).
  2. Send the bank statement PDF. If it is password-protected, send the password in the next message.
  3. Wait for the FinHealth score + 4–6 line summary.
  4. Open the site only if you want the full PDF, Hinglish chat, or a second statement.

Why this can grow users fast

Friends forward WhatsApp numbers. Family groups share “try this”. A website link often dies in chat. A WhatsApp bot that answers with a score is something people actually try. That is the 10x adoption path — not more ads, fewer steps.

Same banks as the web product: HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, and more — Indian PDF formats, INR, UPI, EMI.

2. Smart Budgeting & Savings Alerts — From Past Score to Next Action

A FinHealth score answers: How was last month? Alerts answer: What should I do this week?

Examples StatementIQ will send (WhatsApp or in-app):

  • “Your Swiggy/Zomato spending this month is 20% above your budget.”
  • “Your EMI is due in the next 5 days. Please keep enough balance.”
  • UPI / cash-out spikes vs your usual month
  • Salary credit late vs last 3 months

This turns StatementIQ from a past-analysis tool into a proactive financial assistant — still simple, still India-first, still no heavy “wealth app” clutter.

Who this helps

  • Salaried people who want EMI + food-delivery control without Excel
  • Anyone applying for a loan or credit card who needs a clean money picture
  • NBFC / lending teams who want borrowers to stay current (B2B on the site)

FAQ

Will I need to install another app?

No. WhatsApp you already have. The full report stays on statementiq.in.

What if my PDF has a password?

Send the PDF, then send the password in chat. The bot needs it only to open the file and score it.

Will alerts spam me?

No. A few high-signal messages: overspend vs budget, EMI due soon, unusual bounce — not daily noise.
