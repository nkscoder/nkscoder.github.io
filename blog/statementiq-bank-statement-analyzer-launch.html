Bank statements are long and confusing. StatementIQ turns them into a clear money-health score, plain-English insights, and Hinglish AI chat — built for Indian banks, UPI, EMI, and INR formats.

What Is StatementIQ?

StatementIQ is a bank statement analyzer for India. You register free, upload a statement, and see an instant FinHealth preview. Unlock the full report for AI chat, deep analysis, and PDF download.

  • 15+ banks — HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, and more
  • PDF or Excel — auto bank detection
  • Hinglish AI chat — ask about salary, EMI, Swiggy spend, bounces
  • 30-day auto-delete — data privacy by default
  • No subscription — pay only when you unlock a new statement

How It Works (4 Steps)

  1. Register free — no card needed for the first report
  2. Upload statement — PDF or Excel from your bank
  3. See FinHealth score — 0–100 score + plain-English insights
  4. Unlock full report — AI chat + PDF (first unlock free)

Pricing (Simple)

  • First report: ₹0 (new personal accounts)
  • After that: ₹49 per statement · 30-day access

Who Is It For?

  • Anyone who wants salary / EMI / bounce clarity without Excel pain
